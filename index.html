What it does

A logbook that does the sums you were taught to do on paper.

Nothing here is invented. The app uses the parameters from your care team, applies them consistently, and shows its working.

01 / Readings

Straight from your CGM

Glucose arrives from Dexcom, Libre or any connected app — through Apple Health on iPhone, and Health Connect on Android. Nothing to retype.

02 / Meals

Swiss food, already in there

Zopf, Rösti, Birchermüesli — 160 of them, with carbohydrates and glycemic index. Search Open Food Facts and USDA for the rest, or add your own.

03 / Doses

Your ratios, applied

Carbohydrate ratio and correction factor come from your doctor and live in settings. The app divides; it never decides.

The part nobody shows you

See the curve before you inject.

Most apps record what happened. DiabetesFit also shows the shape of it — how your insulin and your meal overlap, minute by minute, for your brand of insulin.

  • Move the injection earlier or later and watch the glucose curve answer.
  • Separate profiles for Fiasp, Lyumjev, NovoRapid, Humalog and Apidra.
  • A second explorer for long-acting insulin: see what a late basal dose does to the next 24 hours.
For the appointment

Numbers your doctor can read.

Time in range, variability, estimated A1C and the trends behind them — then a PDF report or a CSV file to take with you. Your own foods and saved meals export too, so the work you put in stays yours.

Privacy

There is nowhere for your data to go.

No account to create, no servers to trust, and no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking code of any kind. Your logbook lives on your phone and backs up to your own iCloud or Google account — never to us, because there is no us to send it to.
