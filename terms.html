5. Data Storage and Sync

5.1 Local Storage

All data is stored locally on your device — using Apple's SwiftData framework on iPhone and iPad, and a local database on Android. We have no access to it and operate no servers of our own.

5.2 Sync and Backup

On iPhone and iPad, your data syncs through your own iCloud account, managed entirely by Apple's CloudKit. This is not a paid feature; it is part of how the app stores data. We have no access to your iCloud data.

On Android there is no live sync. Your data is included in Android Auto Backup, which copies it to your own Google account. This is a backup rather than a continuous sync, and it is controlled by your device's backup settings. Your Premium purchase is deliberately excluded from that backup — it is restored from the store you bought it in, not from a backup file.

5.3 Health Platform Integration

On Android, DiabetesFit reads blood glucose from Health Connect so that readings from your CGM app do not have to be retyped. Access is read-only, it is requested the first time you use the feature, and it can be withdrawn at any time in Health Connect. The app never writes to Health Connect.

On iPhone and iPad, DiabetesFit integrates with Apple HealthKit to:

  • Read continuous glucose monitor (CGM) data
  • Display glucose readings in the app

HealthKit permissions are requested when you first use the app. You can modify these permissions in iOS Settings at any time.
